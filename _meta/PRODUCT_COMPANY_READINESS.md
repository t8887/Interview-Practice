# Product-Company Readiness Matrix

Scored against a **Google-tier bar for SDE-2/Senior Full-Stack (backend-lean), ~6 YOE, Node/TS/React/AWS** (`CLAUDE.md`).

**Scoring rule, applied without exception:** 0 = no exposure · 1 = aware · 2 = can explain basics · 3 = solves common interview problems independently · 4 = strong practical + theoretical, handles follow-ups · 5 = expert with trade-offs + implementation.

**A score of 3+ requires solving/implementation artifacts in the repo — not notes about the topic.** Where the only evidence is explanatory prose or a "final answer, no derivation" pattern, the score is capped at **2.5** and marked **⚠️ unverified**, regardless of how well-written the prose is. Every score below cites the specific file(s) that justify it.

## Resume-vs-Prep Mismatch

Claims made in `CLAUDE.md`'s project-story anchors, the candidate's self-intro across `12-Company/` files, or the behavioral-story file, that the current prep material either cannot defend under follow-up or actively contradicts.

### 1. The OpenSearch "2s→200ms" migration — the single biggest mismatch found

This is `CLAUDE.md`'s own flagship metric, and it is the fact most likely to be spoken, under follow-up pressure, in an actual interview. Across the files analyzed, it is stated with **at least 8 mutually inconsistent values**:

| `10-Interview-Prep/01-stories-behavioral.md` Story 2 — the file meant to be spoken almost verbatim | 5s → ~50ms |
| `encora-L2-backend-engineer.md` Q8 | 2s → 200ms (matches canon) |

**Risk:** an interviewer who cross-references the resume/LinkedIn against a rehearsed answer, or simply asks a natural follow-up twice in different words, can catch this candidate contradicting themselves on their own headline achievement. **Fix before anything else in this file** — resolve to CLAUDE.md's "2s→200ms" (already independently agreed by 4+ files) and correct every outlier.

### 2. `11-AI-Risk-Assistant-Project/README.md` — completed-work claim for an unbuilt project

The file's own "Interview Talking Points" section is written entirely in completed past tense — *"I built an AI Risk Assessment Assistant as a portfolio project that demonstrates..."* — while the same file's "Features to Implement (Priority Order)" section makes clear this is an unbuilt scaffold, and **no `ai-risk-assistant/` directory exists anywhere in the repo.** If rehearsed and used verbatim, this is a false claim of completed work, not "getting ahead of yourself." Reword to present/in-progress tense, or gate past-tense talking points behind a tracked build-completion status in `_meta/state.json`.

### 3. "5+ years" vs. CLAUDE.md's "~6 YOE" — a raw, checkable resume fact

`CLAUDE.md`'s own profile line states "~6 YOE." Reconstructing the timeline from the candidate's own project dates (as laid out across `capgemini-L2-interview-prep.md`, `coforge-tech-lead/coforge-prep.md`, and `guardian-india/guardian-prep.md`) independently arrives at ~5.5–6 years — rounding to "~6," not "5+." Yet **6 of 8+ `12-Company/` files reviewed** (Capgemini, CitiusTech, both Deloitte files, HCLTech, Infosys, HealthSystems) independently say "5+ years" or "~5 years," while only `EY-HDFC-Ergo-Apigee/interview-prep.md` and `guardian-india/guardian-prep.md` correctly match "~6 yrs." This is exactly the kind of number an interviewer sanity-checks against a resume or LinkedIn profile — worth reconciling to one figure across every file before any of them is used live.

### 4. The Synechron/Asurion-Japan short stint — five incompatible framings

A ~3-month tenure is independently framed **five different ways** across the folder:
- `coforge-tech-lead/coforge-prep.md`: "the role's actual work turned out to be quite different from what was scoped"
- `infosys-L2-interview-prep.md`: "I was in Asurion Japan project where I was onboarding... It was a short engagement"
- `guardian-india/guardian-prep.md`: "the actual project reality didn't match... I made a fast, honest call"
- `bolttech-multivision-interview-prep.md` STAR story #7: frames the *same period* as active, successful contribution ("Contributing within the ramp window")
- `tcs-L2-hr-preparation.md`: merges it into an undifferentiated "Year 5+" block with no short-tenure framing surfaced at all

Four of the five are compatible in substance (still onboarding, made a fast call); the fifth (bolttech) directly contradicts them by describing the same weeks as productive contribution. Short tenures are a standard thing interviewers probe specifically — the candidate needs one private, consistent account, not five independently-generated ones that could be compared if the same interviewer somehow saw two.

### 5. Salary figures — two files invent a ~2× outlier with no basis

`13-Salary-Negotiation/salary-negotiation-mastery.md` establishes current CTC ≈₹12L, target ₹18-22L — independently corroborated by Deloitte (₹21.4-23.6L), CitiusTech (₹18-21L), HealthSystems/Setu (~₹20-25L). Two files break this pattern by roughly **doubling** it with no reconciliation: `tcs-L2-hr-preparation.md` ("current CTC 40 LPA... target 45-50 LPA") and `persistent-aws-backend-developer.md` ("Recommended range: 40-45 LPA"). If either script were internalized and spoken, it would produce an immediate, credibility-destroying mismatch against every other document the candidate has — including a resume an HR interviewer may already have open.

### 6. VAPT-hardened APIs — the story is real, its technical backup is disconnected

`10-Interview-Prep/01-stories-behavioral.md` Story 4 covers the VAPT compliance work in generic terms ("input validation... security headers"). The specific, defensible mechanics that would survive a security-focused follow-up — least-privilege IAM design, the Secrets Manager rotation contract, JWT algorithm-confusion-attack prevention — all exist correctly elsewhere in the repo (`15-AWS-Services/security.md`, `07-System-Design/01-auth-caching-api.md`'s gap) but are never cross-linked into the story that needs them. Not a knowledge gap — a wiring gap that would show up the moment an interviewer asks "what specifically did you find?"

`12-Company/infosys-L2-interview-prep.md` states an exact past appointment ("11-04-2026, 10:00 AM IST," ~4 months before the current date, plus a specific candidate ID and building location). `encora-L2-backend-engineer.md` and `persistent-aws-backend-developer.md` are both dated May 2026, also ~3 months stale. Not a defense-risk in the way the items above are, but these three files are currently indistinguishable from active prep in the folder structure — worth archiving so the "what's actually live right now" surface area stays honest, per the same staleness concern the original `_meta/INVENTORY.md` raised for the folder as a whole.
